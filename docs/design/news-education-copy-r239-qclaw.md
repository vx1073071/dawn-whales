## 2. PRICE MOVE ATTRIBUTION — USER GUIDE

### 2.1 What Is Price Move Attribution?
When a stock or crypto in your portfolio moves more than 5% in a day, the system automatically:
1. Detects the unusual price movement
2. Searches recent news mentioning that asset
3. Uses AI to identify the most likely cause
4. Shows you a one-sentence explanation with confidence level

### 2.2 Attribution Badge States

| State | Badge | Example |
|-------|-------|---------|
| **AI Found Cause** | ✅ Green | "📰 Earnings beat estimates by 23%. Revenue growth accelerated." · Confidence: 85% |
| **Multiple Causes** | 🟡 Yellow | "📰 3 possible factors: (1) Analyst upgrade + PT raise (2) Sector rotation (3) Short squeeze" |
| **No Clear Cause** | ⚪ Gray | "📰 No specific news found. May be technical factors or market-wide movement." |
| **Analyzing** | 🔵 Blue pulse | "🔍 Analyzing recent news for NVDA +5.2%..." |
| **Error** | 🔴 Red | "⚠️ Unable to complete analysis. Tap to retry." |

### 2.3 Confidence Levels Explained

```
📊 Confidence Score Guide

🟢 HIGH (80-100%)
  Multiple independent sources confirm the same cause.
  Actionable: High confidence attribution you can trade on.

🟡 MEDIUM (60-79%)
  One clear source but limited corroboration.
  Use as directional signal. Verify with your own analysis.

🟠 LOW (40-59%)
  AI's best guess based on correlation, not causation.
  Treat as hypothesis only. Do not trade on this alone.

🔴 UNKNOWN (<40%)
  No clear cause identified. May be noise or technicals.
  No actionable information.
```

### 3.3 Tooltip Glossary

| Term | Tooltip |
|------|---------|
| **Sentiment** | AI-estimated market reaction direction based on historical impact of similar news |
| **Impact Score** | 1-5 scale: 1=minor mention, 3=moderate market mover, 5=potential black swan |
| **Confidence** | How certain the AI is about this classification. Based on source reliability and corroboration |
| **Relevance** | How directly this news affects your specific holding. High = same company, Medium = sector, Low = broad market |
| **Freshness** | Time since publication. News decays: <1h = 100%, 1-6h = 80%, 6-24h = 50%, >24h = 10% |
