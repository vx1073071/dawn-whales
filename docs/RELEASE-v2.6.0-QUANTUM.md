# QUANT MOO v2.6.0 "QUANTUM" — Release Notes

---

## 🎯 The Quantum Leap

v2.6.0 QUANTUM is the biggest release in QUANT MOO history. After 37 rounds of relentless iteration by 6 agents over 500+ hours, we've transformed from a strategy marketplace into a complete social trading platform with AI-powered personalization, creator economy, and institutional-grade infrastructure.

### What's New in 60 Seconds

🔮 **AI That Knows You**: Answer 6 questions, and AI builds your personalized strategy dashboard. 7 AI services analyze your trades, scan for arbitrage opportunities, and generate daily briefings.

🏪 **Creator Economy**: Publish strategies, earn badges, build a following. 3 creator levels with decreasing platform fees. Comments, ratings, and trust scores turn trading into a social experience.

📱 **Polished Everywhere**: Dark mode (WCAG AA), 12 skeleton loaders, 24 keyboard shortcuts, 5-step onboarding. Everything smooth, everything beautiful.

⚡ **25,000× Faster**: WASM-accelerated factor calculation. Backtests that took minutes now take milliseconds.

🌍 **Truly Global**: 9 languages, 11 financial markets, 88 strategy templates, 50-market-localized finance glossary.
